\section{Research Experience}
\entrymini{Physics-residual Flow Matching under distribution shift}
          {\href{https://amiteshpuri.github.io/reports/fm-ood-residual.pdf}{[Report]}\ \
           \href{https://github.com/AmiteshPuri/fm-ood-residual}{[GitHub]}}
{\itshape Independent research report}\par\vspace{2pt}
\begin{tight}
  \item Ran a controlled comparison of a plain flow-matching baseline, a weighted physics residual (RFM), and the conflict-free PBFM formulation on a next-step generative task, across 5 seeds, 7 sampling budgets, and 4 graded distribution-shift families, with 95\% bootstrap confidence intervals.
  \item In distribution, the one-line weighted residual ($\lambda = 0.005$) lowered relative L2 from 0.0148 to 0.0094 (about 37\%) and the PDE residual from 13.2 to 7.5 (about 44\%), with MMD down about 63\%, at a matched 16-step sampling budget.
  \item Found the parameter-free PBFM statistically indistinguishable from the baseline, and reported that the residual's advantage did not carry over under stronger initial-condition and forcing shifts, where it often reversed.
\end{tight}
\vspace{5pt}
\entrymini{When does a geodesic probability path help latent flow matching?}
          {\href{https://amiteshpuri.github.io/reports/fm-paths.pdf}{[Report]}\ \
           \href{https://github.com/AmiteshPuri/FM-paths}{[GitHub]}}
{\itshape Independent research report}\par\vspace{2pt}
\begin{tight}
  \item Tested whether a straight-line or a geodesic probability path changes latent flow matching, holding the data, model, optimiser, and seed fixed across three converged, seed-matched runs.
  \item Reached a controlled null result: the two paths were statistically indistinguishable on accuracy and, once seed noise was removed, on the spectrum, and an early single-seed lead for the straight line did not survive repetition.
  \item Isolated the cause with an ablation: geodesic geometry helped only on manifold-structured data (geodesic penalty 0.63) and hurt off it (2.17), and measured the real VQ-VAE latents to be off the sphere (radius CV $\approx$ 0.27, intrinsic dimension $\approx$ 5.5 against an ambient 32).
\end{tight}
