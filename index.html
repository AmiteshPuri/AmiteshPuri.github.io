Research focus

Learning physical systems without losing their structure

The recurring question across my work: how can machine learning build useful representations and generative models of complex physical systems while preserving the mathematical and structural properties those systems obey — and remaining reliable when resolution, parameters, and distribution change?

01 Scientific machine learning

Learning PDE solution operators and dynamics; neural operators; physics-informed objectives that encode governing equations.

02 Generative modelling

Flow matching, diffusion, and latent generative models; discrete and continuous representations of scientific fields.

03 Mathematical structure

Dynamical systems, numerical methods, and geometric or structural constraints as first-class modelling ingredients.

04 Reliability

Distribution shift, robustness, and uncertainty quantification — when and why learned models fail out of distribution.
